DataGrid Pivot

Two financial years of software bookings, 4,224 records across eleven territories, four product lines, four sales channels and three customer segments, with a docked smart-pivot-panel beside the Grid. Drag a field between the Rows / Columns / Values wells to re-pivot, right-click a field for the same moves without dragging, or use the panel's Filters tab to narrow the bookings. A filter runs before aggregation, so the totals are recomputed from what survives rather than masked.

Pivot is a native view, so everything else keeps working: drag-select a range of cells, copy with Ctrl+C, resize and reorder columns. Row groups are ordinary tree rows, so the expanders come for free, and the column axis becomes collapsible banded headers - click a year to fold it to its total.
